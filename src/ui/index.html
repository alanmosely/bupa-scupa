    Archive help

    Statements needing attention

      Check these PDFs in your archive. If a statement format has changed, keep the files and try a newer SCUPA release. Your master is kept unchanged when parsing fails.

      Keep diagnostics and statements private. Share a synthetic example when reporting an issue.

      Backups and recovery

      Close SCUPA before restoring a backup. Keep a copy of the current master, then restore a chosen file from master/backups to master/claims.csv. Back up the entire archive, including PDFs and household settings.

      Your privacy and storage

      SCUPA keeps names, health statements, payment details, backups and exports as readable files. It does not encrypt the archive. Protect the folder using your device’s account controls, encryption and backups.

      Cloud backup or sync services may upload a folder they manage. CSV exports and agent responses also contain personal information.

      SCUPA has no built-in analytics or AI uploads. Sync connects to Bupa’s website and the services it loads. Passwords and MFA stay in the manual browser login flow. Temporary browser files may remain after a crash.

      If you give an AI assistant access to your records, its service may receive them. Removing the app leaves your archive in place.
