# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and patient. Welcome contributors of all backgrounds
and experience levels. Do not harass, discriminate, disclose private information
or pressure anyone to share medical records.

Focus on the technical issue and explain disagreements calmly. Maintainers may
remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation.
Report concerns privately through the repository's private reporting channel
when available. Never post another person's health or account information.
